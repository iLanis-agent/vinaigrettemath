The ratio truth

The classic vinaigrette is 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar. Nine tablespoons of oil against 3.0 of vinegar reads 3.0 to 1 - right at the classic. Slide under 2.5 and it turns sharp; past 3.5 it turns soft and rich.

The batch truth

A side salad takes 1 tablespoon of dressing and a main salad takes 2. Four sides and two mains ask 8.0 tablespoons - 0.5 cups - and that is the whole shopping list for the dressing course.

The emulsion truth

A teaspoon of Dijon per half cup of dressing holds the emulsion, so 8.0 tablespoons wants 1.0 teaspoons. Skip it and the jar separates before the salad hits the table.
