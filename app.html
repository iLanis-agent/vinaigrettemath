Vinaigrette Math

Boundary: illustrative arithmetic only Labeled kitchen norms - 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar, 1 tablespoon of dressing per side salad and 2 per main, a teaspoon of Dijon per half cup to hold the emulsion. Your recipe and your taste win every tie.

The ratio truth

The classic vinaigrette is 3 parts oil to 1 part vinegar.

The batch truth

A side salad takes 1 tablespoon of dressing, a main salad takes 2.

The emulsion truth

A teaspoon of Dijon per half cup of dressing holds the emulsion.
